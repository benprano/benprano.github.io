  \item Built streaming inference pipelines for low-latency prediction, per-patient personalisation and interpretable outputs under clinical constraints (PyTorch, Unity integration, HPC).
  \item Created three models for joint imputation and prediction on sparse time series (\textbf{TF-JEPA, DyFAIP, FASTER}; 3 papers under review), evaluated on MIMIC-III, PhysioNet-2012 and ETT.
  \item Designed \textbf{FASTER-P}, a single recurrent model that handles every missingness type and rate (MCAR/MAR/MNAR, 20–80\%), benchmarked on identical masks against BRITS, GRU-D, SAITS, TimesNet and ImputeFormer; extended it into an EDM diffusion imputer.
  \item Developed \textbf{calibrated residual diffusion}, which adds calibrated uncertainty to any imputer in 1–2 network passes: \textbf{90.9\%} raw coverage of a 90\% interval versus 77–79\% for standard samplers, and \textbf{31–41\% lower CRPS} than full diffusion on the same backbone (preliminary).
\end{cvitems}
